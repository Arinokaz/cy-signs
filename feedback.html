Feedback — Cyprus Road Signs Quiz

We value your feedback! If you've found a bug, have a feature suggestion, or want to share your experience with our Cyprus Road Signs Quiz app, please use this form to contact us.

Why Your Feedback Matters

  • Improve the app — Your suggestions help us add new features and fix issues
  • Better learning experience — Report confusing signs or translations
  • Community driven — Help other learners by sharing your experience

What to Include in Your Feedback

  • Bug reports: describe what happened and what you expected
  • Feature requests: explain how the feature would help you learn
  • Translation issues: specify the sign and the correct translation
  • General comments: any thoughts on improving your learning experience
